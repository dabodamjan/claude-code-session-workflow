## Reply rule: "tldr"

When my message contains "tldr" (or "tl;dr"), reply short AND plain. Hard ceiling: 10 lines or 130 words, whichever comes first, counted on prose only (code blocks and quoted text do not count). Lead with the real-world thing: what broke, what changed, what it means for people. Ticket numbers, branch names and tool names attach to a plain-language noun, never replace it. Cut the reasoning, the caveats and the how; keep the what and the so-what. If you are correcting something you told me earlier, say so in one plain sentence, no hedge. End with what I still have to do: list every pending item that is mine. Write "nothing needs you" only when that list is empty, never next to an item that needs me. If it does not fit, give the single most important thing and stop; I will ask for more.
